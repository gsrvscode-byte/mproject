9. GENERAL RULES THAT INCREASE CALL RATE
---------------------------------------------
- Apply on the FIRST day a job is posted — visibility drops fast after that.
- Keep resume to 1 page (2 max) — recruiters spend ~10-15 seconds per resume.
- Match resume keywords to the exact job description before applying —
  many portals/ATS auto-score resumes by keyword match.
- Set Naukri profile visibility to "visible to all recruiters."
- Respond to recruiter messages within a few hours — fast responders
  get prioritized for the next round.
- Keep a WhatsApp/email professional and check it daily — many recruiters
  message instead of calling first.
