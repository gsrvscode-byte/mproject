You can say:

> **"I have worked on Health and Travel Insurance applications where I was mainly involved in developing the complete customer-to-policy journey using Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB/MySQL, and third-party APIs."**

Then explain the flow:

**Customer Form → Premium → KYC → Proposal → Razorpay → Policy**

> "Initially, the customer enters their basic information such as name, DOB, contact details, member details, travel details in case of Travel Insurance, and health-related information for Health Insurance."

* DOB/age
* Destination
* Travel dates

* Plan
* Zone
* Add-ons

**Health:**

* Proposer details
* Member details
* DOB/age
* Relationship
* Medical information
* Previous policy/portability details
* Sum insured
* Plan

### 2. Premium calculation

Then explain the important part:

> "Once the required information is filled, our backend validates the data and prepares a request according to the insurer's API requirements. We call the third-party premium API with parameters such as age, members, plan, zone, coverage, travel dates, and other product-specific information. The insurer returns the premium, and we display or use that premium for the next stage."

You can give an example:

> "For Travel Insurance, if the customer selects a particular zone, plan, travel duration, and traveller age, these values are sent to the insurer API and the premium is calculated based on those parameters."

---

### 3. KYC integration

Then:

> "After the customer information is validated, we integrate third-party KYC APIs. We send the required customer information to the KYC service and handle different responses such as successful verification, failed verification, pending status, and retry."

This is a good point to mention that **you didn't just call the API—you handled the complete response flow.**

---

### 4. Proposal generation

> "After premium and KYC are successfully completed, we prepare the proposal request using the customer and policy information collected from the previous stages. We call the insurer's proposal API and store the proposal details and status in our database."

---

### 5. Razorpay payment

For Razorpay, explain it technically:

> "For payment, I integrated Razorpay. From the backend, we create a Razorpay order with the required amount. The frontend uses the order details to initiate the payment. After payment, we handle the Razorpay response and verify the payment on the backend. We maintain statuses such as success, failed, pending, and retry."

You can mention:

**Razorpay flow:**

`Create Order → Payment → Callback/Response → Verify Payment → Update DB → Policy Issuance`

---

### 6. Policy issuance

Finally:

> "Once payment is successfully verified, we trigger the policy issuance process through the insurer API. We receive the policy details/document and update the proposal and policy status in our database."

So your complete explanation becomes:

```text
Customer Form
      ↓
Backend Validation
      ↓
Premium API
      ↓
KYC API
      ↓
Proposal API
      ↓
Razorpay Payment
      ↓
Payment Verification
      ↓
Policy Issuance API
      ↓
Policy Generated
```
