PART 2 — HOW TO TALK ON THE CALL (MEDIUM-PACED, PROPER EXPLANATION)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Medium-paced" means: not rushed (nervous), not overly slow/robotic
(rehearsed). Aim for how you'd explain something to a smart colleague
who's genuinely interested — natural pauses, but always moving forward.

HOW TO OPEN THE CALL:
When they say "tell me about yourself" — don't start instantly like
you're reciting. Take one breath, then start with PART 1's summary,
said naturally (not word-for-word). Then add ONE line about what
you're looking for right now, and stop. That's it — 45-60 seconds
total, not more.

HOW TO EXPLAIN ANYTHING THEY ASK (universal structure):
  1. Context — one sentence on the situation ("At Policyboss, insurer
     PDFs came in inconsistent formats...")
  2. Your action — what YOU did, using "I," at medium pace, giving 2-3
     concrete details (tech names, specific decisions) so it doesn't
     sound vague
  3. Result — one sentence on the outcome
  4. Stop talking — let them ask the next question

WHAT TO DO WHEN THEY ASK A FOLLOW-UP QUESTION (this is where medium
pace matters most):
  - Pause for a second before answering, even if you know it — this
    alone makes you sound more thoughtful and less rehearsed.
  - Answer only what was asked first, then optionally add one extra
    relevant detail. Don't dump everything you know about the topic —
    that reads as nervous over-explaining, not depth.
  - If it's a "why" question (why did you choose Redis, why MongoDB
    over MySQL here), always give the REASON, not just the fact —
    reasoning is what shows real experience over memorized answers.

WHAT QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM (don't just wait to be asked "any
questions?" — ask 1 naturally mid-conversation if it fits):
  - "What does the day-to-day look like for this role — more feature
    work or more system design?"
  - "What's the current tech stack the team is using?"
  - "What's the biggest technical challenge the team is dealing with
    right now?"
These make you sound engaged, not just being interviewed passively.

TONE CHECK — medium pace, in practice:
  - Rushed/nervous: short breathless sentences, no pauses, jumping
    between points.
  - Overly slow/robotic: reciting memorized lines exactly, no natural
    variation.
  - Medium (target): 1-2 second pause between ideas, natural word
    choice that varies slightly each time, stops cleanly after
    answering.

a follow-up question instead of you guessing what to say next.

PART 2 — CALL PE KAISE BAAT KARO (MEDIUM-PACED, PROPER EXPLANATION)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Medium-paced" ka matlab hai: na jaldi (nervous), na zyada slow/robotic
(rehearsed). Aisa explain karo jaise kisi smart colleague ko explain
kar rahe ho jo genuinely interested hai — natural pauses, lekin hamesha
aage badhte rehna.

CALL KAISE OPEN KARO:
Jab wo bolein "tell me about yourself" — turant recite karna shuru mat
karo. Ek breath lo, phir PART 1 ki summary se start karo, naturally
bolke (word-for-word nahi). Phir ek line add karo ki abhi kya dhoond
rahe ho, aur ruk jao. Bas — total 45-60 seconds, zyada nahi.

JO BHI PUCHHEIN USE KAISE EXPLAIN KARO (universal structure):
  1. Context — situation pe ek sentence ("Policyboss mein, insurer
     PDFs inconsistent formats mein aate the...")
  2. Tumhara action — tumne kya kiya, "I" use karke, medium pace pe,
     2-3 concrete details deke (tech names, specific decisions) taaki
     vague na lage
  3. Result — outcome pe ek sentence
  4. Bolna band karo — unhe agla question poochne do

FOLLOW-UP QUESTION AAYE TO KYA KARO (yahin medium pace sabse zyada
matter karta hai):
  - Answer dene se pehle ek second pause karo, chahe pata hi kyun na
    ho — sirf isse tum zyada thoughtful aur kam rehearsed lagte ho.
  - Pehle sirf wahi answer karo jo poochha gaya hai, phir optionally
    ek extra relevant detail add karo. Topic ke baare mein sab kuch
    dump mat karo jo pata hai — ye nervous over-explaining lagta hai,
    depth nahi.
  - Agar "why" question hai (Redis kyun choose kiya, MySQL ki jagah
    MongoDB kyun yahan), hamesha REASON do, sirf fact nahi — reasoning
    hi real experience dikhata hai, memorized answers se alag.

UNSE KYA QUESTIONS POOCHNE HAIN (bas "any questions?" poochne ka wait
mat karo — agar conversation mein fit ho to naturally beech mein 1
poochho):
  - "Is role mein day-to-day kaisa hota hai — zyada feature work ya
    zyada system design?"
  - "Team abhi kaunsa tech stack use kar rahi hai?"
  - "Abhi team ka sabse bada technical challenge kya hai?"
Ye tumhe engaged dikhata hai, sirf passively interview diye jaane ki
jagah.

TONE CHECK — medium pace, practically:
  - Rushed/nervous: short breathless sentences, koi pause nahi, points
    ke beech jump karna.
  - Overly slow/robotic: exact memorized lines recite karna, koi
    natural variation nahi.
  - Medium (target): ideas ke beech 1-2 second ka pause, natural word
    choice jo har baar thoda alag ho, answer ke baad cleanly ruk jaana.
